Projects

Longer write-ups for these are still to come — each links to a page that isn't built yet.

  • PhD research · DMF Lab, 2023–present Lightweighting with Hollow Structures

    My PhD is about getting topology optimisation to produce genuinely hollow, shell-like parts — combining phase-field and level-set methods rather than filling components with solid material or lattices. This project includes both interactive tools on the site.

    → Try the PCLS demonstratorAlso see: GD Setup Tool
  • Visiting research · Carnegie Mellon University, 2025 GPU-Based Optimisation for Topology Design

    A research visit to CMU's Integrated Design Innovation Group, building machine-learning surrogate models to speed up GPU-based branch-and-bound solvers — enough to crack benchmark problems that had previously resisted solution. Paper in preparation.

  • Research Engineer · NDT Lab, 2024–2025 Additively Manufactured Microfluidics

    Cheap, 3D-printed microfluidic devices for labs that can't afford cleanroom fabrication. I built a Fusion 360 add-in that automates scaffold design, then ran a two-day workshop on the manufacturing method in Bangkok.

  • Research internship · DMF Lab, 2022 Information-Sharing in Distributed Manufacturing Networks

    How much information do decentralised, agent-based manufacturing networks actually need to share to stay coordinated? I simulated these networks in AnyLogic and built a classification system for their communication patterns.

  • Internship · Cambustion Ltd., 2021 Roadside ANPR Trigger

    A Raspberry Pi and Arduino rig that reads number plates from roadside gas analysers in real time, plus a companion test rig for measuring fuel-injector leakage through a cloud-linked spreadsheet.

  • Group project · Final year, 2023 The Canute Braille Reader

    A redesign of a multi-line braille e-reader's motors and control system, done with an industry partner. My part was the mechanical redesign and the feasibility study that followed.

  • Individual project · Third year, 2022 System Identification for Power Electronics

    Tuning a PI controller for a buck converter using nothing but least-squares system identification — an automated tuner that got the step response within a few percent of the target.

  • Co-founder & technical manager · 2021–2023 Bristol Racing

    Co-founding a student society that designs and builds small electric vehicles for endurance racing, and two years leading its technical programme — from workshop access to lap times at Castle Combe.
